Professional Biography

Tewodros Solomon is a clinical pharmacist and university lecturer with a Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) and a Master of Science in Clinical Pharmacy (MSc). He serves in the Pharmacy Education and Service Directorate at Wollo University, Ethiopia. His professional interests include pharmacy education, clinical pharmacy practice, evidence-based pharmacotherapy, student mentorship, and continuous improvement in teaching and learning.
